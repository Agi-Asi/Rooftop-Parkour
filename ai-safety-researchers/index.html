    About this project

    A fact-checked who's who of the people working to keep AI safe.

    What it is, how it was checked, and how to get around it.

    What this is

    The Guardians of AI is a curated, fact-checked who's who of 97 AI safety and alignment researchers and public voices: lab safety teams, academics, independent organisations, policy people and the field's pioneers.

    It holds 383 verbatim sourced quotes, 336 interviews and talks, 317 verified videos and 7 original motion videos.

    Methodology

    Verbatim quotes only

    No paraphrases. Every quote carries a link to its source.

    Links checked

    Every source link was requested on 19 Oct 2026. YouTube videos were verified through YouTube oEmbed.

    Current roles

    Roles were checked against 2025–26 sources and carry an "as of" date, since they change fast.

    Sourcing score

    How strong the sources are, not how right the person is.

    50% source quality, 25% link health, 15% source breadth, 10% no unverified flags. 85 and up is green, 70 to 84 amber, below 70 grey.

    Uncertain items

    Anything that could not be confirmed is flagged unverified rather than left out or stated as fact.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is AI alignment?

    Alignment is the work of making AI systems pursue the goals their designers and users actually intend, and stay honest and controllable as they grow more capable. AI safety is the wider field around it, from interpretability and evaluations to governance.

    Who is included and why?

    97 people whose research, leadership or public voice shapes AI safety and alignment: lab safety teams, academics, independent organisations, policy and governance figures, critics and the field's historical pioneers. Inclusion means influence on the debate, not endorsement of anyone's views.

    How are quotes verified?

    Every quote is verbatim and links to its source. Each link was requested on 19 Oct 2026, sources are graded by type, and YouTube videos were confirmed through YouTube oEmbed. Anything that could not be confirmed is flagged "unverified".

    What does the sourcing score mean?

    It measures how strong a person's sources are, not how right they are: 50% source quality, 25% link health, 15% source breadth and 10% for having no unverified flags. 85 and up shows green, 70 to 84 amber, below 70 grey.

    Why do some videos open on the full site?

    Some previews and embedded copies of this page cannot stream video. When playback is not available, the player offers a link to the same video on the full site instead.

    Is this affiliated with any lab?

    No. It is an independent project, curated by AI AGI Engineer, and is not affiliated with, funded by or endorsed by any AI lab or organisation it covers.
